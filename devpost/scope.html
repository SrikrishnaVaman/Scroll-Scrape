Project Scope · Approved

Scroll Scrape

A personal space for places discovered on social media, where AI turns your saves into a timed plan for the day and your photos into a photobook.

Author: Krishna (solo) Devpost Learn · Build With AI: Basics 21 October 2026

The unique kernel

Saved places become plans. Existing save-for-later features stop at storage. Scroll Scrape's AI builds an itinerary using only the places you saved, ordered by real opening hours and travel time and fitted to what you feel like doing that day.

How it flows

Who it's for

Primary user

Young adults anywhere in the world who save hangout spots from Instagram (restaurants, cafés, parks, arcades) to visit later with friends.

The problem today

Saves are hard to use when it's time to plan. People scroll back through folders, take screenshots, or rebuild plans from memory. The author lost saved folders after hitting Instagram's collection limit.

The core loop

1Save

Paste a post link with its caption or a screenshot. AI identifies the place; you confirm or correct it.

2Ask

Describe the outing you want, such as "a relaxed Saturday, affordable food, close to public transport".

3Plan

Get an itinerary from your own saves, with opening hours, travel times and a labelled crowd estimate.

4Remember

Add photos after the outing. They are matched to the places you visited and form a photobook.

What "working" looks like

A demo video under three minutes, presented by the author:

  1. The problem: hitting Instagram's saved-folder limit and losing saves.
  2. Save: an Instagram post about a place is added, and the identified place is confirmed.
  3. Plan: with several places saved, a preference produces an itinerary with real opening hours and travel times.
  4. Remember: outing photos appear against the correct places in a photobook.
Key demo moment: a collection of unrelated saved posts becomes one ordered, timed plan for the day.

Boundary: now, later, cut

  • NowPlace capture: link plus caption or screenshot; AI identifies the place, and the user confirms or corrects it.
  • NowSaved places list.
  • NowAI itinerary: preference input, real opening hours and travel times, and a labelled crowd estimate.
  • NowPhotobook (built last): photos matched by embedded location and date; places marked visited.
  • LaterRecipe vault with AI sorting, shopping list and steps.
  • LaterOne-tap saving from Instagram's share menu (as ReciMe does); needs a native app and permitted access to posts.
  • LaterPlace extraction from a reel's audio.
  • LaterAdditional platforms such as TikTok.
  • LaterBackup and export.
  • LaterSharing a plan with friends.
CutAutomatic import of Instagram saved folders

Instagram does not allow outside apps to read a user's saves; places are added by pasting a link.

CutAutomatic photo library scanning

Requires a native mobile app; the user selects photos instead.

CutLive crowd data

No official live source is available to apps; replaced by a clearly labelled AI estimate.

CutRe-hosting a post's video or images

Likely conflicts with platform terms; Scroll Scrape stores the link and place details only.

CutOther explored ideas

Late-night food finder, cab fare comparison, budget coach and group-chat planner were explored and set aside.

Alignment with judging criteria

Design

One coherent journey (save, plan, remember) rather than disconnected features.

Potential Impact

A specific, first-hand problem for a clearly defined audience.

Innovation

Turns saves into a timed, preference-aware itinerary built only from your own places.

Presentation

A short, linear demo with a clear before-and-after moment.
