Screen by screen

Home and city search

Dock: Home

What you see

  • A flat illustration of the Earth with the flamingo waiting.
  • A city search bar at the top and a shortcut to Memories.
  • Searching a city: the flamingo flies there, then City View opens.

What if

  • First use: the flamingo invites you to save your first place, with a shortcut to Add.
  • City with no saves: the flamingo says you haven't saved anything there yet.
  • Animation runs late: a simple transition to City View is acceptable.

How we'll know it works

  • Searching a city with a space shows the flight, then its City View.
  • Searching a city with no saves shows the flamingo's message and an Add shortcut.

Saving a place

Dock: Add · develops scope.md › The Core Loop (Save)

What you see

  • Two options: Save a post and Add photos.
  • Paste the post link and caption (comments optional), or drop in a screenshot.
  • A confirmation card: place name, type, city, address from map data, post link. Edit anything, then confirm.

What if

  • AI can't tell the place: the flamingo asks you to paste comments or add a screenshot of them.
  • Still unclear: the flamingo asks a few short questions (name, city or area, type of place).

How we'll know it works

  • A real post's link and caption produce the correct name, type and city.
  • A screenshot of the same post gives the same card.
  • Edits on the card are what get saved.
  • An unclear post can still be saved via comments or questions.

City spaces and the Saved screen

Dock: Saved

What you see

  • Every saved place belongs to a space for its city.
  • First place in a new city: "You don't have a space for Singapore yet. Shall I create one?"
  • Saved lists each city space with its number of places.

What if

  • You decline: no space is created, nothing is saved, and you're back on the card to change the city or cancel (assumption).
  • Second place in that city: added without asking again.

How we'll know it works

  • A new city triggers the prompt; approving creates the space with the place in it.
  • Declining creates nothing.
  • Tapping a space opens its City View.

City View and Place Details

From a Home search or a space in Saved · develops scope.md › The POC Boundary (Saved places list)

What you see

  • The city map with a pin for each saved place, like searching "convenience stores" on Google Maps, plus a list.
  • Place Details: type and area, opening hours, busy times (AI estimate, labelled), cost per person, rating, post link, visited status and photos.

What if

  • Hours or rating unavailable: shown as "Not available", never guessed.

How we'll know it works

  • Every saved place appears as a pin and in the list.
  • Tapping one opens all its details.
  • Busy times are visibly labelled as an estimate.
  • The post link opens the original post.

Planning with the flamingo

Dock: Plan · develops scope.md › The Unique Kernel and The Core Loop (Ask, Plan)

What you see

  • The flamingo asks what you'd like to do; you answer in plain words.
  • One stop or several, depending on the request, each labelled with its purpose.
  • Ranked by distance, price, waiting time and crowds, rating, and opening hours.
  • Each stop: suggested time, hours, crowd estimate (labelled), cost, travel time from the previous stop.
  • Then: "Want ideas for the rest of your day?"

What if

  • Nothing saved matches: "Not found on your list." Parts that do match are still planned.
  • No city named: the flamingo asks which city.
  • Never suggests a place you haven't saved.

How we'll know it works

  • "Lunch in Singapore, pasta or pizza" returns a saved pizza or pasta place with hours, crowd estimate and cost.
  • "Casual lunch with friends and a cozy dinner" returns two saved places with times and travel between them.
  • Answering the follow-up adds more saved places.
  • Every planned place is in Saved.

Memories

Add › Add photos, or the shortcut on Home · develops scope.md › The Core Loop (Remember) · built last, first to slip

What you see

  • Drop in photos from an outing.
  • Each is matched to a saved place by its location and date; that place is marked visited.
  • Photos grouped by place and date, and shown on each Place Details.

What if

  • No location, or not near any saved place: listed as unmatched; attach it by hand or skip it.

How we'll know it works

  • A photo taken at a saved place appears under that place.
  • That place shows as visited.

Profile

Dock: Profile

What you see

  • Your details and saved spots: places saved, cities, places visited.

Decided

  • Sign-in built now (revised during the spec): email, password and username, plus a demo account for judges.

How we'll know it works

  • Profile shows the counts of places saved, cities and places visited.

Look and feel

  • Light theme, not dark.
  • A blend of ReciMe (clean, photo-led cards) and mymind (calm, white space, uncluttered).
  • A flamingo mascot that guides you.
  • Bright colours, few shades.
  • Mood: easy, cozy, casual.
  • Simple navigation through an always-visible dock.

The sketches here show layout and mood only. Colours and type are settled in the spec.

What the scope said, and what we know now

Scope"View all saved places."
NowSaves are grouped into city spaces the AI creates with your permission, shown on a city map with pins and a list.
Scope"AI identifies the place; the user confirms."
NowA confirmation card you can edit, plus a fallback: paste comments, then answer the flamingo's questions.
Scope"An itinerary from saved places."
NowA flamingo conversation. One stop or several, each matched to its purpose, then an offer to fill the day. "Not found on your list" when nothing fits.
Scope"Photos form a photobook."
NowAdded from the Add screen, shown in Memories and on each place, with unmatched photos attached by hand.

Now, deferred, later, never

  • NowFive-button dock: Home, Saved, Add, Plan, Profile.
  • NowSaving a place: link + caption or screenshot, confirmation, fallback questions.
  • NowCity spaces with permission, and the Saved screen.
  • NowHome: Earth illustration, flamingo, city search, flying animation (simple fallback).
  • NowCity View map and list; Place Details with real hours and ratings and a labelled crowd estimate.
  • NowPlanning with the flamingo from saved places only.
  • NowMemories: photos matched by location and date (built last).
  • NowSign-up and sign-in (email, password, username), Profile, and a demo account.
DeferredRotating 3D globe

Replaced by the flat Earth illustration; too costly for the time available.

DeferredSeparate budget feature

Adds complexity; budget words in a request already cover the demo.

DeferredSearching places you haven't saved

Scraping Google Maps would break Google's terms and blur the "only your saves" idea. A later version could use official map data.

DeferredReading a post's comments automatically

The app can't open Instagram posts; you paste the comments instead.

  • LaterNearby suggestions from official map data, kept separate from your saves.
  • LaterOne-tap saving from Instagram's share menu in a native app.
  • LaterReading a reel's audio.
  • LaterSharing a plan with friends.
  • LaterRecipe spaces using the same engine.
NeverAutomatic import of Instagram saved folders

Instagram does not allow it.

NeverLive crowd data

No official live source; busy times are a labelled estimate.

NeverRe-hosting a post's images or video

The app keeps the link and place details only.

NeverSuggestions from outside your saves (in the proof of concept)

This is the product's identity.

NeverDark theme

The look is light and cozy.

Decisions Krishna made

  • City spaces created with permission: the AI organises, the user stays in control.
  • Sign-in with email, password and username: data kept safely online, one account per person, demo account for judges.
  • Five-button bottom dock for easy navigation.
  • Flamingo mascot for personality and a cozy feel.
  • Flat Earth instead of a 3D globe: the globe is too hard for the time available.
  • Plans only from saved places, with "Not found on your list" otherwise.
  • The plan's shape follows the request, then an offer to plan the rest of the day.
  • No separate budget feature: too complicated for now.
  • Photos added from the Add screen.
  • Unclear posts: comments first, then the flamingo's questions.
  • Light, cozy look inspired by ReciMe and mymind.

Open questions

  • Can waitName of the photo area: "Memories" for now. "MySpace" is an existing social network's name, which the video rules on trademarks may not allow.
