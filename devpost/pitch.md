# Scroll Scrape: One-Page Pitch

**Saved places become plans.** Scroll Scrape turns the restaurants, cafés, parks and arcades you save from Instagram into a timed, ready-to-go itinerary, built only from places you chose yourself.

## The Problem
Young people everywhere discover most of their hangout spots on Instagram and save them "for later." Later rarely comes. When it is time to make plans, those saves are buried in unsorted folders, so people scroll, screenshot, or rebuild plans from memory. Saved collections also have a limit: the author lost entire saved folders after reaching it. The saves exist, but they never become outings.

## The Solution
Scroll Scrape is a personal, limitless space for places found on social media, organised around one journey:

1. **Save.** Paste an Instagram post link with its caption, or drop in a screenshot. AI identifies the place, and the user confirms or corrects it.
2. **Ask.** Describe the day in plain words, for example "a relaxed Saturday, affordable food, close to public transport."
3. **Plan.** Receive an ordered itinerary drawn only from your saves, with real opening hours, real travel times between stops and an estimate of how busy each place usually is.
4. **Remember.** After the outing, add your photos. Each one is matched to the place visited by its location and date, and a photobook is assembled.

## How AI Is Used
| Step | What AI does | What keeps it grounded |
|---|---|---|
| Save | Reads a caption or screenshot and extracts the place name and area | The user confirms or corrects before saving; the place is resolved against real map data |
| Ask | Interprets a free-text request into preferences (mood, budget, transport, timing) | Preferences are shown back to the user |
| Plan | Selects and orders saved places to fit those preferences | Opening hours and travel times come from real map data, not the model |
| Plan | Estimates how busy each place usually is | Clearly labelled as an AI estimate; no live crowd data is claimed |

AI does the reading and reasoning; real data supplies the facts; the user stays in control of what is saved.

## Target Users
**Primary:** young adults anywhere in the world who regularly save food spots and hangout places from Instagram, intending to visit them with friends. They have plenty of ideas and no easy way to act on them.

**Where it works:** the proof of concept is demonstrated in Singapore, where the author lives, but nothing in the design is tied to one city. Scroll Scrape works wherever map data for opening hours and travel times is available.

**Why they would switch:** Instagram's collections store; Scroll Scrape plans. Generic trip planners suggest places the user has never heard of; Scroll Scrape plans only from places the user already wanted to visit.

## What Makes It Different
- **Built from your saves, not a search engine.** Every stop is a place the user personally chose.
- **Grounded, not invented.** Hours and travel times are real; the one estimate is labelled as such.
- **A complete loop.** Save, plan, go, remember, in a single product rather than four apps.
- **Works within platform rules.** No automated access to Instagram saved folders and no re-hosting of media; the user brings the link.

## The Ask
Scroll Scrape answers a question everyone with a full saved folder has asked: *"We saved so many places, so where do we actually go?"* It turns intention into an outing, using AI where it helps and real data where it matters.
